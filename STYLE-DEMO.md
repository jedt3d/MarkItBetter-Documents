# Approved guide style

**Reference** is the selected theme: cool paper, restrained green, IBM Plex
reading text, a persistent desktop chapter rail, and a compact mobile chapter
menu. English, Thai and Japanese share the same chapter structure.

The full guide supersedes the original two-proposal style demo. The old Edition
and comparison URLs redirect to Reference. Screenshots and design-preview images
are deferred while the app's open issues are revised; no empty image slots are
shown to readers.

Heading line-height is at least 1.2 times the font size and 1.4 for Thai/Japanese.
Thai reading text, captions, labels and body emphasis use the looped face.
Source blocks and financial price digits use IBM Plex Mono with script fallbacks.

The major Mermaid chapter stays clearly marked as upcoming. Inline vector
examples have guide-only zoom, pan, Fit All and browser-content maximize controls.
Preferences has its own chapter covering all seven released tabs.
